Car electronics FAQ

Quick answers about compatibility, installation, pricing and affiliate purchases.

What is the difference between Apple CarPlay and Android Auto?

Apple CarPlay is designed for compatible iPhones, while Android Auto is designed for compatible Android phones. Both can provide navigation, calls, messages and media through a compatible car stereo.

How do I check whether a head unit fits my vehicle?

Confirm the vehicle year, make, model, trim, dashboard configuration, factory amplifier, steering-wheel controls and factory camera setup against the compatibility information on the partner's product page before purchasing.

Can I install an Android head unit myself?

Some vehicle-specific and double-DIN systems are designed for straightforward installation, but wiring, dash-kit, amplifier and camera integration requirements vary. Use the supplied instructions and consider a qualified installer when the vehicle has complex factory electronics.
